## Voice and narration disclosure

- **Video narration.** The narration is a synthetic voice, not a human recording. The README and the video plan say it comes from ElevenLabs. **TO CONFIRM (owner):** the ElevenLabs plan used for the final render, that its terms allow this use, and that the video's end card and the submission description say the voice is synthetic ([SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md](SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md)). The committed `demo/voiceover.py` at this commit is an earlier offline Kokoro (Apache 2.0) script; it is not evidence about the final narration.
- **Voice clips in the product.** The two spoken-question clips (`services/api/diocle_api/voice_clips/`) are made with macOS `say`.
- **Speech model.** Meta's Muse Voice Transcribe transcribes speech and does not speak; it is not used for the narration.
- **Video contents.** This repository does not contain the final video; nothing here makes claims about what it shows. **TO CONFIRM (owner):** it opens with the "Built Oct 27 to Nov 4, 2026" card and shows only what was built in the window.
