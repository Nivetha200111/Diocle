## Voice and narration disclosure

- **Video narration.** The submitted video (https://youtu.be/3tEmLs0jrT4) is narrated by a synthetic voice (ElevenLabs), not a Meta model and not part of the product. The script is written in the first person and its account of the author's own experience is the author's. This is disclosed in the README and the video description, not in the video itself. Earlier cuts used the author's own recorded voice; this cut has no music or sound effects.
- **Voice clips in the product.** The two spoken-question clips (`services/api/diocle_api/voice_clips/`) are the author's own recorded voice (Nov 1, 2026), replacing earlier macOS `say` clips.
- **Speech model.** Meta's Muse Voice Transcribe transcribes speech and does not speak; it is not used for the narration.
- **Video contents.** This repository does not contain the final video. From the first frame it says on screen "Built from scratch during this hackathon. GnuCOBOL, the JDK and Meta's models already existed." A "Built Oct 27 - Nov 4, 2026" line is on every terminal slide and a badge is on every product shot; the narration says "I built Diocle during this hackathon" at about 0:29. The narration is captioned on screen. About three quarters of the running time is product footage.
